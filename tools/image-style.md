Art direction for Maranza Simulator (satirical 2D browser life-sim, spiritual heir of the 90s Tabboz Simulator):
- Flat 2D cartoon illustration, bold clean outlines, saturated but slightly muted palette, cel shading with one shadow tone.
- Italian suburban / provincial setting: bars, piazzas, scooters, shopping centres, discos, tracksuits, gold chains, gelled hair. Comedic, affectionate caricature — never mean-spirited, no real brands or real people.
- Readable at small sizes: simple silhouettes, few details, high contrast against the background.
- No text, letters, captions or watermarks in the image unless explicitly requested.
