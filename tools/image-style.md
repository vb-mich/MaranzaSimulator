Art direction for Maranza Simulator (satirical 2D browser life-sim, spiritual heir of the 90s Tabboz Simulator):
- Flat 2D cartoon illustration, bold clean dark outlines, cel shading with one shadow tone.
- Vaporwave palette: hot pink, electric cyan, lilac and sunset orange, neon glows and soft gradient skies; a touch of 90s/Y2K retro (chrome highlights, sunset vibes) that never hurts readability.
- Brainrot meme energy: slightly exaggerated proportions and expressions, swagger poses, "cringe but proud" attitude.
- Italian suburban / provincial setting: bars, piazzas, scooters, shopping centres, discos, tracksuits, gold chains, gelled hair. Comedic, affectionate caricature of behaviours and outfits — never mean-spirited, never ethnic or religious mockery, no real brands, logos or real people.
- Readable at small sizes: simple silhouettes, few details, high contrast against the background.
- No text, letters, numbers, captions or watermarks in the image.
- No real brands, not even by hint: no logo swooshes or three-stripe trims, no monogram or checkerboard luxury prints, no baroque luxury prints, no car-maker crests.
